Our farm

Tencha grown indoors, in Europe, all year.

Tencha is still grown the old way: outdoors, under shade cloth, once a year. We are building a farm to grow it indoors, in Europe, all year round.

  1. i

    More tencha from the same land

    Plants grow in stacked hydroponic layers. No winter and no single spring flush. Harvests run year-round, for up to 6x the yield of field-grown tea from the same footprint.

  2. ii

    AI, light and air, not shade cloth

    Shade is what makes tencha sweet and bright green. In the field it is a cloth and a guess. Indoors it is a recipe: LED and daylight lighting, sensors on every layer, AI climate control, and robotics for handling and harvest.

  3. iii

    Pesticide-free, grown in Europe

    Our halls are sealed from the pests and weather that field tea is sprayed against. Nothing to spray, so we don't. No soil. Real tea plants, grown in the EU, close to the buyers. Not a substitute leaf.

The site

We grow on what industry throws away.

Our growing halls will sit next to a large industrial plant. Its waste heat, hot water up to 110°C, warms the halls. Its CO2 off-gas feeds the plants.

Water, grid power and logistics are already on site. Lower running costs, lower footprint, and a model we can repeat at other plants.

For buyers

Supply that doesn't wait for spring.

Pesticide-free leaf, harvested through the year, not in one flush. Short shipping inside the EU. Leaf you can trace to one farm.

Until our own harvest, we supply matcha from Taiwan, clearly labelled. Buyers who start with us now will be first in line for Theana-grown leaf.
