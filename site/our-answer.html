03 · Our answer

Strategics are looking for three fixes. We are building all three.

Tencha is still grown the old way: outdoors, under shade cloth, once a year. We grow it indoors, in Europe, all year round.

  1. i

    More Tencha from the Same Land

    Plants grow in stacked hydroponic layers. No winter and no single spring flush. Harvests run year-round, for up to 6x the yield of field-grown tea from the same footprint.

  2. ii

    AI, Light and Air, not shade cloth

    Shade is what makes tencha sweet and bright green. In the field it is a cloth and a guess. Indoors it is a recipe: LED and daylight lighting, sensors on every layer, AI climate control, and robotics for handling and harvest.

  3. iii

    Local tea, 100% clean

    No soil, no pesticides. Real tea plants, grown in the EU, close to the buyers. Not a substitute leaf.

The farm

We grow on what industry throws away.

Our growing halls sit next to a large industrial plant. Its waste heat, hot water up to 110°C, warms the halls. Its CO2 off-gas feeds the plants.

Water, grid power and logistics are already on site. Lower running costs, lower footprint, and a model we can repeat at other plants.
