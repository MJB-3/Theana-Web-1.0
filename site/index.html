01 · Why matcha

Calm energy. A ritual a generation chose over coffee.

Caffeine paired with L-theanine, which shade-grown leaf is naturally rich in. Drinkers describe a steady, focused lift without the crash.

You drink the whole leaf, so quality matters. From niche café order to supermarket fridge.
